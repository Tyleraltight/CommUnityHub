#Web Application Development - Assignment 2 CommUnity Hub

## Project Description
My system is a web-based community service and skill-sharing platform. It allows local residents to find, offer, and manage practical everyday services within their neighborhoods.

## Target Audience
Local residents, neighbors, students, and home-based service providers looking for or offering everyday help (such as gardening, computer tutoring, pet walking, and minor household repairs).

## Problem & Needs Addressed
Large commercial hiring platforms often charge high intermediary fees and lack local neighborhood trust. Residents frequently need quick, affordable help from nearby neighbors, while skilled individuals need a simple way to offer their services without complicated business setups.

## Expected Outcome
To provide a clean, easy-to-use local portal where residents can post their skills, browse available services by category, and connect directly with trusted local help in their area.

## Advanced feature in my final project: Payment service

### Purpose
* Commitment Deposit: When two members of the community agree to a skill swap or home-stay exchange, each person puts down a refundable commitment deposit. This helps avoid last minute cancelations and no-shows. Once both parties have confirmed the exchange has taken place the deposit is automatically returned to the user.
* Featured Listing Boost (Optional): Members can pay a small fee to pin their skill offering or urgent exchange request to the top of the community board.
